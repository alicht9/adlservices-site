SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

This policy covers the text messages sent by ADL Services' equipment monitoring system. It applies only to that messaging program.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share mobile phone numbers or text messaging consent with any third party. Mobile numbers are never shared with affiliates for marketing purposes, and are not used for any purpose other than delivering the alerts described below.

What the messages are

ADL Services operates a private telemetry system that monitors equipment — freezer and refrigeration temperature, water leak detection, and similar sensor readings. When a reading crosses a configured threshold, or a sensor stops reporting, the system sends an automated text message to the equipment's owner.

These are operational alerts only. No marketing or promotional messages are sent through this program.

Who receives them

Messages are sent only to phone numbers entered directly into the system by its operator, for equipment that operator is responsible for. Numbers are not collected from any other source, and there is no public sign-up.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies with equipment conditions and is not on a fixed schedule. A typical month produces fewer than five messages. A period of equipment failure produces more: the system sends one message when a problem begins, a reminder no more than once per hour while it continues, and one message when it is resolved.

Data we hold

  • The recipient's mobile number, stored solely to deliver alerts.
  • Sensor readings and the alert history for the monitored equipment.
  • Delivery records retained by our SMS provider, Twilio.

Twilio processes messages on our behalf in order to deliver them to your carrier. This is delivery infrastructure, not data sharing — Twilio does not receive numbers for its own use. See Twilio's privacy policy.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to any message to end them immediately, or HELP for assistance. Opting out stops all alerts, including notifications about equipment failure.
