The program

ADL Services operates an equipment monitoring system that sends automated text message alerts — freezer and refrigeration temperature, water leak detection, and similar sensor readings. When a reading crosses a configured threshold, or a sensor stops reporting, the system texts the equipment's owner.

This program sends operational alerts only. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

How you join

Alerts are sent only to numbers entered directly into the system by its operator, for equipment that operator is responsible for. There is no public sign-up form and numbers are not obtained from any other source.

Message frequency

Frequency varies with equipment conditions and is not on a fixed schedule. A typical month produces fewer than five messages. During an equipment failure the system sends one message when the problem begins, a reminder no more than once per hour while it continues, and one message when it is resolved.

Message and data rates may apply.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to any message to cancel. You will receive a single confirmation, after which no further messages are sent. Reply HELP for assistance, or use the contact details below.

Because this program exists to warn about equipment failure, opting out means you will not be told when monitored equipment stops working.

Delivery is not guaranteed

Messages are delivered on a best-effort basis and depend on your mobile carrier, which may delay or fail to deliver them. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. ADL Services does not guarantee that any alert will be delivered, and this system should not be relied upon as the sole safeguard for equipment where a failure would cause significant loss.

Supported carriers

Alerts are sent to US mobile numbers. Carrier support may change without notice.

Privacy

Mobile numbers are never sold, rented, traded, or shared with third parties. See the SMS Privacy Policy for details.
